Pose training for new photographers

One shot a round. Wait for the pose.

She steps into a base pose, changes one thing at a time, then holds for 2–3 seconds. Somewhere in each round she hits the pose on the reference card. Fire once, at the right moment.

  • The cardThe target pose sits in the bottom-right corner of the viewfinder.
  • The trapEach round has a near-miss: the same pose with one real mistake.
  • CameraLevels 1–3: Auto. Levels 4–6: Aperture priority, set exposure compensation. Levels 7–10: Manual. The light gray studio fools the meter.
  • ReviewAfter each shot: your frame, the reference card and a real example photo, with why the pose works.
  • BonusAny shot over 85 unlocks a magazine Cover Bonus round.
  • ScorePose 80 / exposure 20 (pose only in Auto). No shot in 38.5 s scores 0. Average 70.0 to level up.
