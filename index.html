A fashion photography timing game

Five looks. Three frames each. Wait for the pose.

The model never stops moving. Each look comes with a client brief and a pose from the posing guide. Set the camera before she gets there, then fire when she lands it.

  • ExposureShutter is locked at 1/250. Balance aperture and ISO until the meter reads zero.
  • DepthWide apertures and long lenses melt the background. The brief says which one the client wants.
  • FramingZoom to the shot type: full length, half-length, or tight portrait. Practice only for now; framing is not scored.
  • TimingYou get 8 seconds to dial in the camera before she starts hitting the target pose. The target stays in the corner of the viewfinder. Fire when she matches it. Frames taken mid-move score low.
